Android setup — stop Google and Bixby stealing the headset button
  1. Open Settings, then Apps, then Default apps, then Digital assistant app, and set it to None.
  2. In the Google app, turn off Hey Google detection.
  3. Turn off Bixby Voice wake-up.
  4. In Chrome, allow the microphone for this site.
  5. Come back here, tap Arm headset button, then press your headset button once to test it.

After that setup, one click reaches this page. Long-press is still Android's — it opens whatever Android has set, and this page cannot change that.

How it works, plainly

  • The mic listens in the language you pick above. The translation then auto-detects what was actually said — English words mixed into Spanish or Korean stay in, nothing is stripped out.
  • Words are held until the other person pauses, then translated. If the pause is too short or too long, move the wait slider.
  • The mic goes quiet while the English is playing, so it cannot hear itself or grab background voices.
  • Translation runs through Google Translate with a MyMemory backup. No account, no key. If a translation fetch fails, the page says UNAVAILABLE — it never fakes a translation.
  • It needs your phone mic and a headset, and it is meant to be used in Chrome on Android with the phone near the other person.
  • The log below lives on this page only. Leave the page and it is gone — nothing is sent or stored anywhere.
